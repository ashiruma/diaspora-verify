1. Market Opportunity & Problem Statement 01 / OPPORTUNITY

Over $4.2 Billion USD is remitted annually by Kenyans in the United States, United Kingdom, European Union, and the Gulf region. Approximately 60% of these transfers are earmarked for land acquisition, residential construction, family care, and small business capitalization.

Core Market Dilemma “Diaspora investors rely on relatives, informal caretakers, or self-interested contractors. Without independent physical inspection, over 30% of projects suffer catastrophic variance: phantom cement purchases, misaligned boundary beacons, halted construction with continuous billing, or fictitious medical and business expenses.”

DiasporaVerify solves this with an institutionalized, tech-enabled verification layer: vetted independent professionals (registered architects, surveyors, and engineers) dispatched via a centralized Nairobi operations center, producing tamper-evident, SHA-256 cryptographically hashed dossiers.
